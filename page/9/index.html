Lesson 04 Seeing Hands

Posted on 2017-02-20 In English Reading time ≈ 5 mins.

How did Vera discover she had this gift of second sight?

Several cases have been reported in Russia recently of people who can read and detect colours with their fingers, and even see through solid doors and walls. One case concerns an eleven-year-old schoolgirl, Vera Petrova, who has normal vision but who can also perceive things with different parts of her skin, and through solid walls. This ability was first noticed by her father. One day she came into his office and happened to put her hand on the door of a locked safe. Suddenly she asked her father why he kept so many old newspapers locked away there, and even described the way they were done up in bundles.

Vera’s curious talent was brought to the notice of a scientific research institute in the town of Ulyanovsk, near where she lives, and in April she was given a series of tests by a special commission of the Ministry of Health of the Russian Federal Republic. During these tests she was able to read a newspaper through an opaque screen and, stranger still, by moving her elbow over a child’s game of Lotto she was able to describe the figures and colours printed on it; and, in another instance, wearing stockings and slippers, to make out with her foot the outlines and colours of a picture hidden under a carpet. Other experiments showed that her knees and shoulders had a similar sensitivity. During all these tests Vera was blindfold; and, indeed, except when blindfold she lacked the ability to perceive things with her skin. It was also found that although she could perceive things with her fingers this ability ceased the moment her hands were wet.

📝 文章大意

据报道，俄罗斯最近出现了几例能用手指阅读、辨别颜色，甚至能透过门和墙看东西的人。其中一例是 11 岁的女学生维拉·彼得罗娃：她视力正常，却还能用皮肤的不同部位感知物体。她的父亲最先发现了这种能力——她把手放在锁着的保险柜门上，就说出了里面放着成捆的旧报纸。后来她接受了卫生部特别委员会的一系列测试：隔着不透明的屏幕读报，用肘部辨认乐透卡上的数字和颜色，用穿着袜子和拖鞋的脚辨认地毯下图片的轮廓和颜色。测试时她必须蒙着眼睛，不蒙眼反而感知不到；手一湿，这种能力也就消失了。

标题问题的答案：她把手放在父亲办公室里锁着的保险柜上，说出了里面放着成捆的旧报纸，她的父亲由此发现了她的这种能力。

📖 难句解析

1. Several cases have been reported in Russia recently of people who can read and detect colours with their fingers, and even see through solid doors and walls.

  • 主干：Several cases of people ... have been reported；of people ... 本应紧跟 cases，因为修饰语太长，被移到了谓语后面，避免头重脚轻
  • who 引导的定语从句修饰 people，从句里 read、detect、see through 三个动词并列
  • 译: 最近在俄罗斯报道了几起有人能用手指阅读和辨别颜色，甚至能透过坚实的门和墙看东西的事例。

2. One case concerns an eleven-year-old schoolgirl, Vera Petrova, who has normal vision but who can also perceive things with different parts of her skin, and through solid walls.

  • concern = 涉及，与……有关
  • Vera Petrova 是 schoolgirl 的同位语
  • 两个 who 从句用 but 连接，形成对比：视力正常，但还能用皮肤感知
  • and through solid walls 承接 perceive things，= perceive things through solid walls
  • 译: 其中一例是 11 岁的女学生维拉·彼得罗娃，她视力正常，但她还能用皮肤的不同部位，而且能透过坚实的墙壁感知物体。

3. Suddenly she asked her father why he kept so many old newspapers locked away there, and even described the way they were done up in bundles.

  • why he kept ... 是宾语从句，用陈述语序
  • keep sth locked away = 把某物锁起来，locked away 是宾语补足语
  • the way (that/in which) they were done up 省略了关系词；do up = 包扎、捆好
  • 译: 她突然问父亲为什么把那么多旧报纸锁在里面，甚至还描述了报纸捆扎的方式。

4. Vera’s curious talent was brought to the notice of a scientific research institute in the town of Ulyanovsk, near where she lives…

  • bring sth to the notice of sb = 使某人注意到某事，这里用被动
  • near where she lives：where 引导的从句作介词 near 的宾语
  • 译: 维拉的奇特天赋引起了她家附近乌里扬诺夫斯克市一家科研机构的注意……

5. During these tests she was able to read a newspaper through an opaque screen and, stranger still, by moving her elbow over a child’s game of Lotto she was able to describe the figures and colours printed on it; and, in another instance, wearing stockings and slippers, to make out with her foot the outlines and colours of a picture hidden under a carpet.

  • 全句由三个并列成分组成，都依附于 she was able to：① read a newspaper ② describe the figures ③ （分号后）to make out …，第三部分省略了 she was able
  • stranger still = 更奇怪的是，是插入语
  • printed on it 修饰 figures and colours；hidden under a carpet 修饰 picture，都是过去分词作后置定语
  • make out = 辨认出；它的宾语 the outlines and colours of a picture 被 with her foot 隔开了
  • 译: 在测试中，她能隔着不透明的屏幕读报。更奇怪的是，她把胳膊肘在儿童玩的乐透卡上移动，就能说出印在上面的数字和颜色；还有一次，她穿着长袜和拖鞋，用脚辨认出了藏在地毯下面的一幅画的轮廓和颜色。

6. During all these tests Vera was blindfold; and, indeed, except when blindfold she lacked the ability to perceive things with her skin.

  • indeed 表示进一步强调：事实上
  • except when (she was) blindfold 是省略句，when 后面省略了主语和 be 动词
  • 译: 在所有这些测试中，维拉都被蒙住了眼睛；而且，事实上，只有在蒙住眼睛时，她才能用皮肤感知物体。

7. It was also found that although she could perceive things with her fingers this ability ceased the moment her hands were wet.

  • It was found that ... = 人们发现，it 是形式主语
  • the moment 相当于连词 as soon as，= 一……就……
  • 译: 人们还发现，尽管她能用手指感知物体，但手一湿，这种能力就消失了。

Lesson 03 Matterhorn Man

Posted on 2017-01-20 In English Reading time ≈ 6 mins.

What was the main objective of early mountain climbers?

Modern alpinists try to climb mountains by a route which will give them good sport, and the more difficult it is, the more highly it is regarded. In the pioneering days, however, this was not the case at all. The early climbers were looking for the easiest way to the top, because the summit was the prize they sought, especially if it had never been attained before. It is true that during their explorations they often faced difficulties and dangers of the most perilous nature, equipped in a manner which would make a modern climber shudder at the thought, but they did not go out of their way to court such excitement. They had a single aim, a solitary goal — the top!

It is hard for us to realize nowadays how difficult it was for the pioneers. Except for one or two places such as Zermatt and Chamonix, which had rapidly become popular, Alpine villages tended to be impoverished settlements cut off from civilization by the high mountains. Such inns as there were generally dirty and flea-ridden; the food simply local cheese accompanied by bread often twelve months old, all washed down with coarse wine. Often a valley boasted no inn at all, and climbers found shelter wherever they could — sometimes with the local priest (who was usually as poor as his parishioners), sometimes with shepherds or cheese-makers. Invariably the background was the same: dirt and poverty, and very uncomfortable. For men accustomed to eating seven-course dinners and sleeping between fine linen sheets at home, the change to the Alps must have been very hard indeed.

📝 文章大意

现代登山者专挑有难度的路线，路线越难越受推崇；早期登山者却只想走最容易的路登顶，因为山顶才是他们追求的目标，他们虽然常常遇险，但从不主动寻求刺激。第二段描写早期登山的艰苦：阿尔卑斯山区的村庄贫穷闭塞，旅店又脏又多跳蚤，饭食粗劣，很多山谷连旅店都没有，登山者只能借宿在神父或牧羊人家里。对过惯了舒适生活的绅士们来说，这种转变一定非常艰难。

标题问题的答案：早期登山者的主要目标是登上顶峰（the summit / the top）。

📚 核心单词

单词 音标 词性 含义 文中用法

📖 难句解析

1. Modern alpinists try to climb mountains by a route which will give them good sport, and the more difficult it is, the more highly it is regarded.

  • by a route = 通过某条路线，which 引导定语从句修饰 route
  • good sport = 好的乐趣、刺激，sport 在这里不是”运动”
  • the more ..., the more ... = 越……越……；两个 it 都指 route
  • be highly regarded = 受到高度评价
  • 译: 现代登山运动员总是想找一条能获得极大乐趣的路线登山，路线越艰险，越受到称颂。

2. In the pioneering days, however, this was not the case at all.

  • this was not the case = 情况并非如此，case 指”实情”
  • not ... at all = 根本不
  • 译: 然而，在登山运动的初期，情况却完全不是这样。

3. The early climbers were looking for the easiest way to the top, because the summit was the prize they sought, especially if it had never been attained before.

  • the prize (that) they sought 是省略关系代词的定语从句，sought 是 seek 的过去式
  • had never been attained 是过去完成时的被动语态，指”在他们之前从未被人登上过”
  • 译: 早期的登山者寻找的是通往山顶最容易的路，因为山顶才是他们追求的目标，尤其是以前从未有人登上过的山峰。

4. It is true that during their explorations they often faced difficulties and dangers of the most perilous nature, equipped in a manner which would make a modern climber shudder at the thought, but they did not go out of their way to court such excitement.

  • It is true that ..., but ... = 诚然……，但是……，先让步再转折
  • of the most perilous nature = 极其危险的，of + 抽象名词 作定语
  • equipped in a manner ... 是过去分词短语作状语，说明他们的装备情况
  • make sb shudder at the thought = 让人一想到就发抖，thought 指”想到那种装备”
  • go out of one's way to do = 特意、不怕麻烦地去做；court excitement = 自找刺激
  • 译: 诚然，他们在探险中常常遇到极其危险的困难，而他们的装备简陋得能让现代登山者一想起来就发抖，但他们并不刻意去寻求这种刺激。

5. Except for one or two places such as Zermatt and Chamonix, which had rapidly become popular, Alpine villages tended to be impoverished settlements cut off from civilization by the high mountains.

  • 主干：Alpine villages tended to be impoverished settlements
  • Except for 引出例外；which 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 Zermatt and Chamonix
  • tend to be = 往往是
  • cut off from civilization by the high mountains 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 settlements
  • 译: 除了策马特和夏蒙尼等一两个很快出名的地方以外，阿尔卑斯山区的村庄大都是被高山隔绝、与文明世界不相往来的贫困村落。

6. Such inns as there were generally dirty and flea-ridden; the food simply local cheese accompanied by bread often twelve months old, all washed down with coarse wine.

  • such ... as there were = 仅有的那些……；as 是关系代词，这里暗示旅店数量很少，质量也差
  • 分号后的句子省略了谓语 was：the food (was) simply local cheese ...
  • accompanied by bread = 配着面包；often twelve months old 修饰 bread
  • wash down = （用饮料）把食物冲下去；all 指前面的奶酪和面包
  • 译: 那里仅有的几家小旅店一般都很脏，跳蚤成群；吃的只是当地的奶酪和放了常常有一年之久的面包，用劣质酒送下肚去。

7. Often a valley boasted no inn at all, and climbers found shelter wherever they could — sometimes with the local priest (who was usually as poor as his parishioners), sometimes with shepherds or cheese-makers.

  • boast 本义”自夸”，这里指”拥有（值得夸耀的东西）”，boasted no inn 带有讽刺意味
  • wherever they could 后面省略了 find shelter
  • 破折号后用两个 sometimes with ... 并列，列举借宿的地方
  • 译: 山谷里常常连一家旅店都没有，登山者只好随处找地方住，有时住在当地神父家里（神父通常和他的教民一样穷），有时住在牧羊人或制作奶酪的人家里。

8. For men accustomed to eating seven-course dinners and sleeping between fine linen sheets at home, the change to the Alps must have been very hard indeed.

  • accustomed to eating ... and sleeping ...：过去分词短语修饰 men；be accustomed to 的 to 是介词，后接动名词
  • seven-course dinners = 有七道菜的正餐
  • must have been = 一定曾经……，是对过去的肯定推测
  • 译: 对于那些在家里习惯了吃七道菜的晚餐、睡细亚麻布床单的人来说，到阿尔卑斯山区以后生活上的变化一定非常难以适应。

Lesson 02 Spare That Spider

Posted on 2017-01-10 In English Reading time ≈ 5 mins.

How much of each year do spiders spend killing insects?

Why, you may wonder, should spiders be our friends? Because they destroy so many insects, and insects include some of the greatest enemies of the human race. Insects would make it impossible for us to live in the world; they would devour all our crops and kill our flocks and herds, if it were not for the protection we get from insect-eating animals. We owe a lot to the birds and beasts who eat insects but all of them put together kill only a fraction of the number destroyed by spiders. Moreover, unlike some of the other insect eaters, spiders never do the least harm to us or our belongings.

Spiders are not insects, as many people think, not even nearly related to them. One can tell the difference almost at a glance, for a spider always has eight legs and an insect never more than six.

How many spiders are engaged in this work on our behalf? One authority on spiders made a census of the spiders in a grass field in the south of England, and he estimated that there were more than 2,250,000 in one acre; that is something like 6,000,000 spiders of different kinds on a football pitch. Spiders are busy for at least half the year in killing insects. It is impossible to make more than the wildest guess at how many they kill, but they are hungry creatures, not content with only three meals a day. It has been estimated that the weight of all the insects destroyed by spiders in Britain in one year would be greater than the total weight of all the human beings in the country.

📝 文章大意

蜘蛛是人类的朋友，因为它们消灭了大量昆虫，而昆虫是人类最大的敌人之一：要不是有食虫动物保护，昆虫会吃光庄稼、咬死牲畜，让人类无法生存。鸟兽吃掉的昆虫加起来也只是蜘蛛消灭的一小部分，而且蜘蛛对人类毫无危害。蜘蛛并不是昆虫，一眼就能分辨：蜘蛛有八条腿，昆虫最多六条。一位专家统计，英格兰南部的草地上每英亩有 225 万多只蜘蛛，一年中至少有半年在捕食昆虫。据估计，英国的蜘蛛一年消灭的昆虫，重量超过全国人口的总重量。

标题问题的答案：蜘蛛每年至少有半年在捕食昆虫（at least half the year）。

📖 难句解析

1. Why, you may wonder, should spiders be our friends?

  • 主干是 Why should spiders be our friends?，you may wonder 是插入语
  • should 表示惊讶、不解的语气：（凭什么）竟然要……
  • 开篇设问，下一句 Because ... 直接作答
  • 译: 你可能会感到奇怪，蜘蛛为什么会是我们的朋友呢？

2. Insects would make it impossible for us to live in the world; they would devour all our crops and kill our flocks and herds, if it were not for the protection we get from insect-eating animals.

  • make it impossible for sb to do：it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 to live in the world
  • if it were not for ... = 要不是有……，与现在事实相反的虚拟语气，所以主句用 would make、would devour
  • 分号后的句子是对前半句的具体说明
  • we get from ... 是省略了 that 的定语从句，修饰 protection
  • 译: 要不是有食虫动物保护我们，昆虫会使我们无法在这个世界上生存；它们会吞食我们所有的庄稼，咬死我们的牛羊。

3. We owe a lot to the birds and beasts who eat insects but all of them put together kill only a fraction of the number destroyed by spiders.

  • owe a lot to sb = 多亏了某人，很大程度上归功于某人
  • all of them put together 是 all of them 加过去分词短语，put together = 加在一起
  • only a fraction of = 只是……的一小部分
  • destroyed by spiders 是过去分词作后置定语，修饰 the number
  • 译: 我们很感激吃昆虫的鸟兽，但它们捕食的昆虫全部加起来也只是蜘蛛消灭的昆虫数量的一小部分。

4. Moreover, unlike some of the other insect eaters, spiders never do the least harm to us or our belongings.

  • never ... the least = 一点也不，双重强调否定
  • do harm to sb = 对某人有害
  • 译: 而且，和别的食虫动物不同，蜘蛛对我们和我们的财物丝毫无害。

5. Spiders are not insects, as many people think, not even nearly related to them.

  • as many people think 修饰的是”蜘蛛是昆虫”这个观点，即”不像许多人以为的那样”，不是”正如人们所想”
  • not even nearly related to them 是省略句，= they are not even nearly related to insects
  • not even nearly = 甚至连近亲都算不上
  • 译: 蜘蛛并不像许多人以为的那样是昆虫，它们甚至与昆虫没有什么亲缘关系。

6. One can tell the difference almost at a glance, for a spider always has eight legs and an insect never more than six.

  • tell the difference = 区分，辨别
  • for 是并列连词，表示补充说明理由，比 because 更书面
  • an insect never more than six 省略了 has，= an insect never has more than six
  • 译: 人们几乎一眼就能看出它们的区别，因为蜘蛛总是八条腿，而昆虫的腿从来不超过六条。

7. … he estimated that there were more than 2,250,000 in one acre; that is something like 6,000,000 spiders of different kinds on a football pitch.

  • that is 指代前面整件事，表示换算：这相当于……
  • something like = 大约，相当于 about
  • 译: ……他估计每英亩草地上有 225 万多只蜘蛛，这就是说，一个足球场上大约有 600 万只各种各样的蜘蛛。

8. It is impossible to make more than the wildest guess at how many they kill, but they are hungry creatures, not content with only three meals a day.

  • it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to make ... guess
  • make a guess at = 猜测；impossible to make more than the wildest guess = 除了极粗略地猜一猜，什么也做不到
  • how many they kill 是宾语从句，作 at 的宾语
  • not content with ... 是形容词短语作 creatures 的补充说明
  • 译: 蜘蛛究竟消灭了多少昆虫，我们只能做非常粗略的猜测，但它们是贪吃的动物，不满足于一日三餐。

9. It has been estimated that the weight of all the insects destroyed by spiders in Britain in one year would be greater than the total weight of all the human beings in the country.

  • It has been estimated that ... = 据估计，it 是形式主语
  • 从句主干：the weight ... would be greater than the total weight ...
  • destroyed by spiders in Britain in one year 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 insects
  • would 表示推测
  • 译: 据估计，英国的蜘蛛一年中消灭的昆虫的重量会超过全国人口的总重量。
