Lesson 47 The great escape

Posted on 2018-07-20 In English Reading time ≈ 11 mins.

What is one of the features of modern camping where nationality is concerned?

Economy is one powerful motive for camping, since after the initial outlay upon equipment, or through hiring it, the total expense can be far less than the cost of hotels. But, contrary to a popular assumption, it is far from being the only one, or even the greatest. The man who manoeuvres carelessly into his twenty pounds’ worth of space at one of Europe’s myriad permanent sites may find himself bumping a Bentley. More likely, Ford Escort will be hub to hub with Renault or Mercedes, but rarely with bicycles made for two.

That the equipment of modern camping becomes yearly more sophisticated is an entertaining paradox for the cynic, a brighter promise for the hopeful traveler who has sworn to get away from it all. It also provides—and some student sociologist might care to base his thesis upon the phenomenon—an escape of another kind. The modern traveller is often a man who dislikes the Splendide and the Bellavista, not because he cannot afford, or shuns their material comforts, but because he is afraid of them. Affluent he may be, but he is by no means sure what to tip the doorman or the chambermaid. Master in his own house, he has little idea of when to say boo to a maitre d’hotel.

From all such fears camping releases him. Granted, a snobbery of camping itself, based upon equipment and techniques, already exists; but it is of a kind that, if he meets it, he can readily understand and deal with. There is no superior ‘they’ in the shape of managements and hotel hierarchies to darken his holiday days.

To such motives, yet another must be added. The contemporary phenomenon of car worship is to be explained not least by the sense of independence and freedom that ownership entails. To this pleasure camping gives an exquisite refinement.

From one’s own front door to home or foreign hills or sands and back again, everything is to hand. Not only are the means of arriving at the holiday paradise entirely within one’s own command and keeping, but the means of escape from holiday hell (if the beach proves too crowded, the local weather too inclement) are there, outside—or, as likely, part of—the tent.

Idealists have objected to the practice of camping, as to the package tour, that the traveller abroad thereby denies himself the opportunity of getting to know the people of the country visited. Insularity and self-containment, it is argued, go hand in hand. The opinion does not survive experience of a popular Continental camping place. Holiday hotels tend to cater for one nationality of visitors especially, sometimes exclusively. Camping sites, by contrast, are highly cosmopolitan. Granted, a preponderance of Germans is a characteristic that seems common to most Mediterranean sites; but as yet there is no overwhelmingly specialized patronage. Notices forbidding the open-air drying of clothes, or the use of water points for car washing, or those inviting “our camping friends” to a dance or a boat trip are printed not only in French or Italian or Spanish, but also in English, German and Dutch. At meal times the odour of sauerkraut vies with that of garlic. The Frenchman’s breakfast coffee competes with the Englishman’s bacon and eggs.

Whether the remarkable growth of organized camping means the eventual death of the more independent kind is hard to say. Municipalities naturally want to secure the campers’ site fees and other custom. Police are wary of itinerants who cannot be traced to a recognized camp boundary or to four walls. But most probably it will all depend upon campers themselves: how many heath fires they cause; how much litter they leave; in short, whether or not they wholly alienate landowners and those who live in the countryside. Only good scouting is likely to preserve the freedoms so dear to the heart of the eternal Boy Scout.

📝 文章大意

省钱是人们露营的重要动机，但远不是唯一的、甚至不是最主要的动机：如今露营地上停的常常是名车。露营装备越来越讲究，这在愤世嫉俗者看来是个有趣的悖论。露营还提供了另一种逃避：很多现代旅行者不住豪华旅馆，不是因为住不起，而是因为怕——他们不知道该给门童和女服务员多少小费，也不知道怎么应付领班。露营让他们摆脱了这些顾虑，营地里没有高高在上的管理层。露营还让汽车带来的独立和自由感更加完美：从家门口到度假地，一切都在自己掌控之中，海滩太挤或天气太坏，随时可以离开。理想主义者批评露营让人无法了解当地人，但事实并非如此：度假旅馆往往只接待某一国的游客，露营地却是国际化的，告示用多种语言印刷，饭时德国泡菜的味道和大蒜味争相飘散。有组织的露营会不会让独立露营消亡还难说，这主要取决于露营者自己是否爱护乡村、不惹怒当地人。

标题问题的答案：就国籍而言，现代露营的一个特点是露营地高度国际化，各国游客汇聚一处。

📖 难句解析

1. Economy is one powerful motive for camping, since after the initial outlay upon equipment, or through hiring it, the total expense can be far less than the cost of hotels.

  • Economy 在这里是”节约”，不是”经济”
  • since 引导原因状语从句
  • after the initial outlay upon equipment, or through hiring it 是两个并列的状语：要么先花钱买装备，要么租装备
  • 译: 节约是人们露营的一个重要动机，因为买了装备或租用装备以后，露营的总花费比住旅馆要少得多。

2. But, contrary to a popular assumption, it is far from being the only one, or even the greatest.

  • contrary to = 与……相反
  • far from = 远非，完全不；后接动名词 being
  • one 指 motive
  • 译: 但是，与人们通常的看法相反，节约远不是露营的唯一动机，甚至也不是最重要的动机。

3. The man who manoeuvres carelessly into his twenty pounds’ worth of space at one of Europe’s myriad permanent sites may find himself bumping a Bentley.

  • 主干：The man ... may find himself bumping a Bentley
  • manoeuvre into = 把车开进……
  • twenty pounds' worth of space = 价值 20 英镑的地方，即付了 20 英镑的营位
  • find oneself doing = 发现自己（不知不觉地）在做；Bentley 是宾利豪车，说明露营者并不都是为了省钱
  • 译: 在欧洲众多的固定露营地中，如果你随意把车开进花了 20 英镑租下的营位，你可能会发现自己撞上的是一辆宾利。

4. More likely, Ford Escort will be hub to hub with Renault or Mercedes, but rarely with bicycles made for two.

  • hub to hub = 轮毂挨着轮毂，形容车停得很挤
  • but rarely with ... 省略了 will Ford Escort be hub to hub
  • bicycles made for two = 双人自行车，代表穷游者
  • 译: 更可能的是福特护卫者车和雷诺或奔驰车挨着停放，而很少会和双人自行车停在一起。

5. That the equipment of modern camping becomes yearly more sophisticated is an entertaining paradox for the cynic, a brighter promise for the hopeful traveler who has sworn to get away from it all.

  • That ... 引导主语从句，作整个句子的主语
  • 表语是两个并列名词短语：an entertaining paradox for ... 和 a brighter promise for ...
  • paradox：露营本是为了回归简朴，装备却越来越复杂
  • get away from it all = 摆脱一切（日常的烦恼）
  • 译: 现代露营装备一年比一年复杂，对愤世嫉俗的人来说是一个有趣的悖论，而对那些发誓要摆脱一切的满怀希望的旅行者来说，则是一个更美好的前景。

6. It also provides—and some student sociologist might care to base his thesis upon the phenomenon—an escape of another kind.

  • 主干：It also provides an escape of another kind；破折号之间是插入语
  • might care to do = 也许愿意做
  • base sth upon sth = 以……为根据
  • 译: 它还提供了另外一种逃避——研究社会学的学生也许愿意把这一现象作为他论文的基础。

7. The modern traveller is often a man who dislikes the Splendide and the Bellavista, not because he cannot afford, or shuns their material comforts, but because he is afraid of them.

  • the Splendide and the Bellavista 是两家旅馆的名字，代指豪华旅馆
  • not because ..., but because ... = 不是因为……，而是因为……
  • cannot afford 后面省略了 them
  • 译: 现代旅行者往往不喜欢住”辉煌”和”美景”这类旅馆，不是因为住不起，也不是要避开那里的物质享受，而是因为怕住这样的旅馆。

8. Affluent he may be, but he is by no means sure what to tip the doorman or the chambermaid.

  • Affluent he may be 是倒装，表语提前表示让步，= Although he may be affluent
  • by no means = 决不
  • what to tip 是”疑问词 + 不定式”结构；tip 带双宾语
  • 译: 他可能很有钱，但他根本不知道该给门童或女服务员多少小费。

9. Master in his own house, he has little idea of when to say boo to a maitre d’hotel.

  • Master in his own house 是名词短语作状语，表示让步：虽然在家里是主人
  • have little idea of = 几乎不知道
  • say boo to = 对……说”不”，表示不满或反对；maitre d'hotel 是法语，指餐厅领班或旅馆总管
  • 译: 他在自己家里是主人，却不知道什么时候该对旅馆的总管说个”不”字。

10. Granted, a snobbery of camping itself, based upon equipment and techniques, already exists; but it is of a kind that, if he meets it, he can readily understand and deal with.

  • Granted = 诚然，先让步，后面用 but 转折
  • based upon ... 是过去分词短语，修饰 snobbery
  • it is of a kind = 它属于那一种，of + 名词 作表语
  • that 引导定语从句修饰 kind，中间插入了 if he meets it；that 作 understand 和 deal with 的共同宾语
  • 译: 诚然，露营本身已经存在着一种以装备和技术为标准的势利现象，但即使碰到了，他也能很容易地理解和应付。

11. There is no superior ‘they’ in the shape of managements and hotel hierarchies to darken his holiday days.

  • 'they' 加引号表示”他们”这群高高在上的人
  • in the shape of = 以……的形式，表现为
  • to darken ... 是不定式作定语，修饰 ‘they’
  • 译: 这里没有旅馆管理人员和等级森严的员工这类高人一等的”他们”来使他的假日蒙上阴影。

12. The contemporary phenomenon of car worship is to be explained not least by the sense of independence and freedom that ownership entails.

  • be to be explained = 可以被解释，be to do 表示可能
  • not least = 尤其，特别
  • that ownership entails 是定语从句，修饰 the sense；entail = 使必然产生
  • 译: 当代人对汽车的崇拜，尤其可以用拥有汽车所带来的独立感和自由感来解释。

13. Not only are the means of arriving at the holiday paradise entirely within one’s own command and keeping, but the means of escape from holiday hell (if the beach proves too crowded, the local weather too inclement) are there, outside—or, as likely, part of—the tent.

  • Not only 放在句首，前半句部分倒装：are the means ...
  • within one's own command and keeping = 完全在自己的掌控之中
  • 括号里第二个分句省略了 proves：the local weather (proves) too inclement
  • outside—or, as likely, part of—the tent：outside 和 part of 共用宾语 the tent；as likely = 同样可能；指车就停在帐篷外，或者帐篷本身就是车的一部分
  • 译: 不仅去度假胜地的交通工具完全由自己掌控，而且逃离度假”地狱”的工具（如果海滩太挤或当地天气太坏）就在帐篷外面，或者同样可能就是帐篷的一部分。

14. Idealists have objected to the practice of camping, as to the package tour, that the traveller abroad thereby denies himself the opportunity of getting to know the people of the country visited.

  • object to sth that ... = 以……为理由反对……；that 从句说明反对的理由
  • as to the package tour = as (they have objected) to the package tour，省略句
  • thereby = 因此，由此；deny oneself sth = 放弃某物，不给自己某物
  • the country visited = 所访问的国家，过去分词后置
  • 译: 理想主义者反对露营，正如反对包价旅游一样，理由是出国旅行的人因此失去了了解所访问国家人民的机会。

15. Insularity and self-containment, it is argued, go hand in hand.

  • it is argued 是插入语 = 有人认为
  • go hand in hand = 密切相关，同时存在
  • 译: 有人认为，与世隔绝和自我封闭是密切相关的。

16. Granted, a preponderance of Germans is a characteristic that seems common to most Mediterranean sites; but as yet there is no overwhelmingly specialized patronage.

  • a preponderance of = 占多数的……
  • be common to = 为……所共有
  • as yet = 到目前为止
  • overwhelmingly specialized patronage = 绝大多数顾客来自某一特定国家的情况
  • 译: 诚然，德国人占多数似乎是地中海地区大部分露营地的共同特点，但至今还没有一个露营地是专门接待某一国游客的。

17. Whether the remarkable growth of organized camping means the eventual death of the more independent kind is hard to say.

  • Whether ... 引导主语从句
  • the more independent kind = the more independent kind of camping
  • 译: 有组织的露营的显著增长是否意味着较为独立的露营方式的最终消亡，现在还很难说。

18. But most probably it will all depend upon campers themselves: how many heath fires they cause; how much litter they leave; in short, whether or not they wholly alienate landowners and those who live in the countryside.

  • 冒号后面三个并列的宾语从句，具体说明 depend upon 的内容
  • in short = 总之，引出最后一个概括性的从句
  • alienate = 使疏远，使不友好
  • 译: 但最有可能的是，这一切将取决于露营者自己：他们会引起多少起荒野火灾，留下多少垃圾；总之，取决于他们是否会完全疏远土地所有者以及住在乡村的人。

19. Only good scouting is likely to preserve the freedoms so dear to the heart of the eternal Boy Scout.

  • scouting = 童子军活动；这里指露营者要守规矩、讲公德
  • so dear to the heart of ... 是形容词短语后置修饰 freedoms；be dear to one's heart = 为某人所珍爱
  • the eternal Boy Scout = 永远的童子军，指心中始终保有童子军精神的露营者
  • 译: 只有良好的童子军作风才可能保住那些永远的童子军们所珍视的自由。

Lesson 46 Hobbies

Posted on 2018-07-10 In English Reading time ≈ 9 mins.

Who, according to the author, are ‘Fortune’s favoured children’?

A gifted American psychologist has said, ‘Worry is a spasm of the emotion; the mind catches hold of something and will not let it go.’ It is useless to argue with the mind in this condition. The stronger the will, the more futile the task. One can only gently insinuate something else into its convulsive grasp. And if this something else is rightly chosen, if it is really attended by the illumination of another field of interest, gradually, and often quite swiftly, the old undue grip relaxes and the process of recuperation and repair begins.

The cultivation of a hobby and new forms of interest is therefore a policy of the first importance to a public man. But this is not a business that can be undertaken in a day or swiftly improvised by a mere command of the will. The growth of alternative mental interests is a long process. The seeds must be carefully chosen; they must fall on good ground; they must be sedulously tended, if the vivifying fruits are to be at hand when needed.

To be really happy and really safe, one ought to have at least two or three hobbies, and they must all be real. It is no use starting late in life to say: “I will take an interest in this or that.” Such an attempt only aggravates the strain of mental effort. A man may acquire great knowledge of topics unconnected with his daily work, and yet get hardly any benefit or relief. It is no use doing what you like; you have got to like what you do. Broadly speaking, human beings may be divided into three classes: those who are toiled to death, those who are worried to death, and those who are bored to death. It is no use offering the manual laborer, tired out with a hard week’s sweat and effort, the chance of playing a game of football or baseball on Saturday afternoon. It is no use inviting the politician or the professional or business man, who has been working or worrying about serious things for six days, to work or worry about trifling things at the weekend.

As for the unfortunate people who can command everything they want, who can gratify every caprice and lay their hands on almost every object of desire—for them a new pleasure, a new excitement is only an additional satiation. In vain they rush frantically round from place to place, trying to escape from avenging boredom by mere clatter and motion. For them discipline in one form or another is the most hopeful path.

It may also be said that rational, industrious, useful human beings are divided into two classes: first, those whose work is work and whose pleasure is pleasure; and secondly, those whose work and pleasure are one. Of these the former are the majority. They have their compensations. The long hours in the office or the factory bring with them as their reward, not only the means of sustenance, but a keen appetite for pleasure even in its simplest and most modest forms. But Fortune’s favoured children belong to the second class. Their life is a natural harmony. For them the working hours are never long enough. Each day is a holiday, and ordinary holidays, when they come, are grudged as enforced interruptions in an absorbing vocation. Yet to both classes, the need of an alternative outlook, of a change of atmosphere, of a diversion of effort, is essential. Indeed, it may well be that those whose work is their pleasure are those who most need the means of banishing it at intervals from their minds.

📝 文章大意

（本文节选自丘吉尔的《业余爱好》。）一位美国心理学家说，忧虑是情绪的痉挛，头脑抓住某件事就不肯放。此时和头脑讲道理没有用，意志越强，越是徒劳，只能悄悄塞给它另一样东西；如果选对了，原先的紧张就会慢慢松开，身心开始恢复。所以培养爱好对公众人物极为重要，但这不是一天能办成的事，就像播种一样，要精心挑选、细心照料。一个人至少要有两三种真正的爱好，到晚年才临时培养是没有用的：不是做你喜欢的事，而是要喜欢你做的事。人可以分为三类：累死的、愁死的、闲死的；对于那些想要什么就有什么的人，新的乐趣只会让他们更加厌腻，对他们来说，某种形式的约束才是最有希望的出路。勤勉有用的人又可分为两类：工作是工作、娱乐是娱乐的人，以及工作与娱乐合二为一的人。后者才是”命运的宠儿”，但他们恰恰最需要时不时把工作从脑子里赶出去。

标题问题的答案：”命运的宠儿”是那些工作和娱乐合二为一的人。

Lesson 07 Bats

Posted on 2018-01-30 In English Reading time ≈ 5 mins.

In what way does echo-location in bats play a utilitarian role?

Not all sounds made by animals serve as language, and we have only to turn to that extraordinary discovery of echo-location in bats to see a case in which the voice plays a strictly utilitarian role.

To get a full appreciation of what this means we must turn first to some recent human inventions. Everyone knows that if he shouts in the vicinity of a wall or a mountainside, an echo will come back. The further off this solid obstruction, the longer time will elapse for the return of the echo. A sound made by tapping on the hull of a ship will be reflected from the sea bottom, and by measuring the time interval between the taps and the receipt of the echoes, the depth of the sea at that point can be calculated. So was born the echo-sounding apparatus, now in general use in ships. Every solid object will reflect a sound, varying according to the size and nature of the object. A shoal of fish will do this. So it is a comparatively simple step from locating the sea bottom to locating a shoal of fish. With experience, and with improved apparatus, it is now possible not only to locate a shoal but to tell if it is herring, cod, or other well-known fish, by the pattern of its echo.

It has been found that certain bats emit squeaks and by receiving the echoes, they can locate and steer clear of obstacles—or locate flying insects on which they feed. This echo-location in bats is often compared with radar, the principle of which is similar.

📝 文章大意

动物发出的声音并不都是语言，蝙蝠的回声定位就是一个声音纯粹起实用作用的例子。要理解这一点，可以先看人类的发明：对着墙或山喊话会听到回声，障碍物越远，回声返回得越慢。在船体上敲击，声音会从海底反射回来，测量敲击与回声之间的时间间隔就能算出海的深度，回声探测仪由此诞生。每个固体都会反射声音，鱼群也一样，所以从探测海底到探测鱼群只差一步，如今甚至能根据回声的模式判断出是鲱鱼还是鳕鱼。人们发现某些蝙蝠会发出尖叫声，靠接收回声来避开障碍物或捕捉飞虫，这种回声定位常被拿来和原理相似的雷达相比。

标题问题的答案：蝙蝠发出尖叫声，通过接收回声来确定方位，从而避开障碍物、找到作为食物的飞虫。

1. Not all sounds made by animals serve as language, and we have only to turn to that extraordinary discovery of echo-location in bats to see a case in which the voice plays a strictly utilitarian role.

  • Not all ... 是部分否定：并非所有……都……
  • made by animals 是过去分词作后置定语，修饰 sounds；serve as = 起……的作用
  • have only to do ... to do = 只要……就能……
  • in which 引导定语从句修饰 a case
  • 译: 并不是所有动物发出的声音都起语言的作用。我们只要看一看蝙蝠回声定位这一非凡的发现，就能明白在某些情况下，动物的叫声纯粹是起实用作用的。

2. To get a full appreciation of what this means we must turn first to some recent human inventions.

  • To get ... 是不定式作目的状语
  • appreciation 在这里是”充分理解”，不是”欣赏”或”感激”
  • what this means 是宾语从句，this 指上句所说的蝙蝠回声定位
  • 译: 要充分理解这句话的意思，我们必须先看一看人类最近的一些发明。

3. The further off this solid obstruction, the longer time will elapse for the return of the echo.

  • the + 比较级 ..., the + 比较级 ... = 越……越……
  • 前半句省略了 is：The further off this solid obstruction (is)
  • elapse 指时间流逝，主语是 time
  • 译: 障碍物越远，回声返回所需的时间就越长。

4. A sound made by tapping on the hull of a ship will be reflected from the sea bottom, and by measuring the time interval between the taps and the receipt of the echoes, the depth of the sea at that point can be calculated.

  • made by tapping on ... 是过去分词短语，修饰 sound
  • by measuring ... 是方式状语，修饰后面的 can be calculated
  • between A and B：A 是 the taps，B 是 the receipt of the echoes
  • 主句是被动语态：the depth ... can be calculated
  • 译: 敲击船体发出的声音会从海底反射回来，测出敲击声和收到回声之间的时间间隔，就能算出该处海水的深度。

5. So was born the echo-sounding apparatus, now in general use in ships.

  • 完全倒装句，正常语序是 So the echo-sounding apparatus was born；主语较长，所以放到后面
  • now in general use 是 apparatus 的补充说明；in general use = 普遍使用
  • 译: 回声探测仪就这样诞生了，现在已普遍用于船上。

6. Every solid object will reflect a sound, varying according to the size and nature of the object.

  • varying ... 是现在分词短语，逻辑主语是被反射的声音，表示声音会随物体而变化
  • according to = 根据；nature 在这里是”性质”
  • 译: 每个固体都会反射声音，反射的声音随物体的大小和性质而不同。

7. With experience, and with improved apparatus, it is now possible not only to locate a shoal but to tell if it is herring, cod, or other well-known fish, by the pattern of its echo.

  • it is possible to do：it 是形式主语，真正的主语是两个不定式
  • not only to locate ... but (also) to tell ... 连接两个不定式
  • tell if ... 的 tell = 辨别，不是”告诉”
  • by the pattern of its echo 修饰 tell，表示凭什么来辨别
  • 译: 凭借经验和改进的仪器，现在不仅能确定鱼群的位置，而且能根据回声的特征辨别出是鲱鱼、鳕鱼，还是其他人们熟悉的鱼。

8. It has been found that certain bats emit squeaks and by receiving the echoes, they can locate and steer clear of obstacles—or locate flying insects on which they feed.

  • It has been found that ... = 人们发现，it 是形式主语
  • that 从句里有两个分句：certain bats emit squeaks 和 by receiving ..., they can ...
  • locate and steer clear of 共用宾语 obstacles
  • on which they feed 是定语从句修饰 insects，来自 feed on = 以……为食
  • 译: 人们发现，某些蝙蝠能发出尖叫声，通过接收回声，它们能确定障碍物的位置并避开障碍物，或者找到它们作为食物的飞虫。

9. This echo-location in bats is often compared with radar, the principle of which is similar.

  • be compared with = 与……相比
  • the principle of which 引导非限制性定语从句，= and its principle，which 指 radar
  • 译: 蝙蝠的回声定位常被拿来与雷达相比，两者的原理是相似的。

Lesson 06 The Sporting Spirit

Posted on 2018-01-20 In English Reading time ≈ 5 mins.

How does the writer describe sport at the international level?

I am always amazed when I hear people saying that sport creates goodwill between nations, and that if only the common peoples of the world could meet one another at football or cricket, they would have no inclination to meet on the battle field. Even if one didn’t know from concrete examples (the 1936 Olympic Games, for instance) that international sporting contests lead to orgies of hatred, one could deduce it from general principles.

Nearly all the sports practised nowadays are competitive. You play to win, and the game has little meaning unless you do your utmost to win. On the village green, where you pick up sides and no feeling of local patriotism is involved, it is possible to play simply for the fun and exercise: but as soon as the question of prestige arises, as soon as you feel that you and some larger unit will be disgraced if you lose, the most savage combative instincts are aroused. Anyone who has played even in a school football match knows this. At the international level, sport is frankly mimic warfare. But the significant thing is not the behavior of the players but the attitude of the spectators; and, behind the spectators, of the nations who work themselves into furies over these absurd contests, and seriously believe—at any rate for short periods—that running, jumping and kicking a ball are tests of national virtue.

📝 文章大意

（本文选自乔治·奥威尔的同名文章。）有人说体育能增进各国之间的友谊，只要各国百姓能在球场上相见，就不会想在战场上相见。作者对此深感惊讶：即使不看 1936 年奥运会这样的实例，单凭常理也能推断出国际比赛会引发疯狂的仇恨。如今的体育几乎都以竞争为目的，在村头草地上随意分队比赛还可以只图娱乐，可一旦牵涉到荣誉，人们最野蛮的好斗本能就会被激发出来。在国际层面，体育简直就是模拟战争；更值得注意的是观众以及他们背后的国家，他们为这些荒唐的比赛大动肝火，还真把跑、跳、踢球当成对民族美德的检验。

标题问题的答案：作者认为国际体育比赛无异于模拟战争（frankly mimic warfare）。

1. I am always amazed when I hear people saying that sport creates goodwill between nations, and that if only the common peoples of the world could meet one another at football or cricket, they would have no inclination to meet on the battle field.

  • hear sb doing = 听到某人在说（强调正在说的过程）
  • 两个并列的 that 宾语从句：that sport creates ... 和 that if only ... they would ...
  • if only = 要是……就好了，后接过去式（could meet），第二个从句用 would：对现实的虚拟，作者认为这想法不现实
  • inclination to do = 做某事的意愿，have no inclination to = 不想
  • the common peoples 的 peoples 是”各民族”，common 是”普通的”
  • 译: 每当听到有人说体育能增进国与国之间的友好，说只要世界各国的普通人能在足球场或板球场上相见，他们就不会想在战场上相见，我总是感到惊讶。

2. Even if one didn’t know from concrete examples (the 1936 Olympic Games, for instance) that international sporting contests lead to orgies of hatred, one could deduce it from general principles.

  • Even if + 过去式 ..., one could ...：虚拟语气，表示”即使不知道……也能推断出”
  • one 泛指”人”，正式用法
  • know from ... that ...：know 后面的 that 从句被插入语和 from 短语隔开了，要把 that international ... hatred 接回 know
  • it 指代 that 从句的内容；deduce from = 从……推断
  • orgies of hatred = 仇恨的狂欢，orgy 本指纵酒狂欢，这里带强烈贬义
  • 译: 即使不是从具体事例（比如 1936 年奥运会）中得知国际体育比赛会引发疯狂的仇恨，人们也可以从一般原理推断出来。

  • little 是否定词，has little meaning = 几乎没有意义（a little 才是”有一点”）
  • unless = if not，unless you do your utmost = 除非你竭尽全力
  • do one's utmost to do = 竭尽全力做某事
  • 译: 除非你全力以赴去赢，否则比赛几乎没有意义。

  • where 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 the village green，里面两个分句用 and 连接
  • pick up sides = （随意）分队；involved 是”牵涉其中”
  • it is possible to play 中 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to play
  • simply for 的 simply = 仅仅，注意不是”简单地”
  • 译: 在村里的草地上，大家随便分成两队，不涉及任何地方爱国情绪，这时可以只是为了玩乐和锻炼而比赛。

  • as soon as 连用两次，是排比，加强”一旦……立刻”的语气
  • the question of prestige arises 的 arise 是不及物动词，意思是”出现”，不能用被动
  • some larger unit = 某个更大的集体（球队所代表的学校、城市、国家）
  • will be disgraced if you lose：主句将来时，从句用一般现在时（if 条件句）
  • the most savage combative instincts 的 the most 表示”最”，是真正的最高级
  • be aroused 是被动，arouse = 唤起、激起
  • 译: 但是一旦涉及声誉问题，一旦你觉得输了就会使你和某个更大的集体蒙羞，最野蛮的好斗本能就会被激发出来。

6. But the significant thing is not the behavior of the players but the attitude of the spectators; and, behind the spectators, of the nations who work themselves into furies over these absurd contests, and seriously believe … that running, jumping and kicking a ball are tests of national virtue.

  • 主干：the significant thing is not A but B（重要的不是 A 而是 B）
  • 后面的 of the nations ... 与 of the spectators 并列，都是 the attitude 的修饰语，所以 and 之后 behind the spectators 是插入语
  • who 引导的定语从句里有两个并列谓语：work themselves into furies 和 seriously believe
  • work oneself into a fury = 使自己陷入狂怒；over 表示”为了”
  • running, jumping and kicking a ball 是三个动名词并列作主语，谓语用复数 are
  • 译: 但重要的不是运动员的行为，而是观众的态度，以及观众背后那些国家的态度——它们为这些荒唐的比赛大动肝火，并且认真地相信（至少在短时间内）跑、跳、踢球是对民族美德的检验。

Lesson 05 Youth

Posted on 2018-01-10 In English Reading time ≈ 5 mins.

How does the writer like to treat young people?

People are always talking about ‘the problem of youth’. If there is one — which I take leave to doubt — then it is older people who create it, not the young themselves. Let us get down to fundamentals and agree that the young are after all human beings — people just like their elders. There is only one difference between an old man and a young one: the young man has a glorious future before him and the old one has a splendid future behind him: and maybe that is where the rub is.

When I was a teenager, I felt that I was just young and uncertain — that I was a new boy in a huge school, and I would have been very pleased to be regarded as something so interesting as a problem. For one thing, being a problem gives you a certain identity, and that is one of the things the young are busily engaged in seeking.

I find young people exciting. They have an air of freedom, and they have not a dreary commitment to mean ambitions or love of comfort. They are not anxious social climbers, and they have no devotion to material things. All this seems to me to link them with life, and the origins of things. It’s as if they were, in some sense, cosmic beings in violent and lovely contrast with us suburban creatures. All that is in my mind when I meet a young person. He may be conceited, ill-mannered, presumptuous or fatuous, but I do not turn for protection to dreary cliches about respect for elders — as if mere age were a reason for respect. I accept that we are equals, and I will argue with him, as an equal, if I think he is wrong.

📝 文章大意

人们总在谈论”青年问题”，作者却怀疑这个问题是否存在：即使有，也是老年人制造出来的。年轻人和老年人一样都是人，唯一的区别是年轻人的好日子在前头，老年人的好日子已经过去了，这也许正是症结所在。作者回忆自己少年时只觉得茫然无措，倒巴不得被人当成”问题”，因为这能给人一种身份感。作者觉得年轻人令人兴奋：他们自由，不汲汲于名利和享受，与生命和万物的本源相连。即使年轻人自负无礼，作者也不会搬出”尊敬长辈”的陈词滥调来自保，而是把他们当作平等的人，与他们辩论。

标题问题的答案：作者把年轻人当作与自己平等的人来对待，认为他们错了就和他们平等地争论。

📖 难句解析

1. If there is one — which I take leave to doubt — then it is older people who create it, not the young themselves.

  • 结构: If there is one 条件句 + 插入语 which I take leave to doubt + 强调句 it is ... who ...
  • one = a problem of youth，指代前面的 the problem of youth
  • which 指代整个 if 从句的内容（”是否真有青年问题”），不是指 one
  • take leave to doubt = 恕我冒昧地怀疑，措辞礼貌，语气其实很坚决
  • 译: 如果真有”青年问题”——我可要冒昧表示怀疑——那也是老年人制造出来的，而不是年轻人自己。

2. …the young man has a glorious future before him and the old one has a splendid future behind him: and maybe that is where the rub is.

  • 结构: 两个 has a ... future 平行对照，the old one 的 one = man
  • before him / behind him：未来在前，未来”在身后”，其实是说老年人的好时光已经过去了
  • glorious 与 splendid 同样是褒义词，用在 behind 的一边就带上了讽刺
  • where the rub is = 症结所在（出自《哈姆雷特》”there’s the rub”）；that 指上面这个差别，暗示老年人是嫉妒
  • 译: 年轻人前面有光辉的未来，老年人身后有辉煌的过去——症结也许就在这里。

3. …I would have been very pleased to be regarded as something so interesting as a problem.

  • 结构: would have been pleased 表示对过去的假设（现实中并没有人这样看他）
  • be regarded as = 被看作；so interesting as a problem 中 so ... as 引出比较内容
  • 把”问题”称作”有趣的东西”，是反话：连被当作问题都求之不得，因为说明有人在意
  • 译: 那时要是有人把我看成”问题”这样有意思的东西，我会非常高兴。

4. For one thing, being a problem gives you a certain identity, and that is one of the things the young are busily engaged in seeking.

  • For one thing = 首先（列举理由，后面没有再列）
  • a certain identity = 某种身份、存在感
  • the things (that) the young are ... seeking：定语从句，省略了关系代词 that
  • be engaged in doing = 忙于做某事
  • 译: 首先，成为”问题”会给你一种身份，而寻找身份正是年轻人忙着做的事之一。

5. …they have not a dreary commitment to mean ambitions or love of comfort.

  • 结构: have not a ... 是较老的否定写法，等于 do not have a ...
  • mean 在这里是卑微的、小气的（不是”意思”，也不是”刻薄”）
  • commitment to = 对……的执着，to 是介词，后面接名词
  • mean ambitions or love of comfort 并列，都是 commitment to 的宾语
  • 译: 他们不会沉闷乏味地执着于卑微的野心或对安逸的贪恋。

6. It’s as if they were, in some sense, cosmic beings in violent and lovely contrast with us suburban creatures.

  • as if + 过去式 were：虚拟语气，表示”好像……（其实并不是）”
  • in some sense = 从某种意义上说，是作者的缓和语气
  • in contrast with = 与……形成对照，中间夹了形容词 violent and lovely
  • violent 在此是强烈的，并非”暴力”，与 lovely 并列，是褒义
  • suburban 本指郊区的，这里借指安稳、平庸、缺乏想象力的生活
  • 译: 他们仿佛在某种意义上是宇宙中的生灵，与我们这些郊区居民形成强烈而可爱的对比。

7. …but I do not turn for protection to dreary clichés about respect for elders — as if mere age were a reason for respect.

  • 结构: turn to sth for protection = 求助于某物来保护自己，这里把 for protection 提前了
  • clichés about respect for elders：指”要尊敬长辈”之类的老生常谈
  • as if mere age were a reason for respect：同第 6 句，虚拟语气，表示作者不认同；mere = 仅仅
  • 译: 但我不会躲到”要尊敬长辈”这类陈词滥调后面寻求庇护——好像光凭年纪就值得尊敬似的。
